Brand safety first

Rules we keep
with every partner

Your brand’s reputation matters more than any campaign, so every agreement comes with clear, written rules.

✓We publish your official codes only and never leak a private code.
✓No bidding on your brand name in ads without written permission.
✓No fake countdowns and no exaggerated claims.

Is there a cost to start?

What is the Founding Partner Programme?

We pick a limited number of brands to be our first partners, with premium placement, a custom campaign plan and weekly reports from the team.

Do you list brands you don’t work with?

No. We never show a brand’s name or logo as a partner without a formal agreement or an approved programme.
