Is there a cost to get started?

Not on the commission model: there are no fixed fees; you only pay when sales come in. Hybrid adds a fixed placement fee for a featured slot for 30 days per paid month (home + your store page + the relevant category page). No traffic or sales guarantee.

How is code attribution calculated?

Each brand gets a unique Kobon code. Sales are counted from orders where that code was used, from the store’s own order report or platform (e.g. Salla, Zid, Shopify or your affiliate platform). Link-based tracking only if you provide your own platform; then its cookie window applies.

How is commission set and paid?

The rate depends on the category and margin, and is paid monthly after non-cancelled orders are approved.

What reports do we receive?

Weekly: a Kobon performance report (clicks and code usage on our site). Monthly: a sales & commission statement based on your brand’s order data.

Which tracking platform do you use?

By default a unique Kobon code with your order reports (Salla, Zid, Shopify or your affiliate platform). Link and cookie tracking only if you provide your own platform.

What is the tracking window (cookie window)?

On the code model there is no cookie window; we rely on code use at checkout. If you provide a link platform, its cookie window applies and we agree it in writing before launch.

How long does the agreement last, and can it end?

It’s monthly, and either side can end it with 14 days’ written notice.

Do you work through affiliate networks?

Yes, or on a direct contract, whichever suits you.

Do you work with international stores?

Yes. We work with stores in the Gulf, Egypt and worldwide that ship to the region or globally.

Do you feature brands you don’t work with?

No. A brand’s trade name or its logo only appears as a partner after a formal agreement.
