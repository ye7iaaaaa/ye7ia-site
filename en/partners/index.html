For brands & partnerships

Promoaty is run by a team of marketers with hands-on experience in affiliate and performance marketing. We help brands and online stores reach ready-to-buy shoppers in Saudi Arabia, the GCC and Egypt — and you pay for results.

Email us about a partnership partners@kobon.is-a.si

What we offer

Affiliate partnership (CPA)

We promote your store and earn commission on confirmed sales only — directly or via your network (Admitad, ArabClicks, Boostiny, Awin, Impact and others).

Site-exclusive code

A Promoaty-only code with clear terms and separate tracking, so you can measure our performance precisely.

Seasonal campaigns

White Friday, 11.11, Ramadan, Saudi National Day — search-ready seasonal pages where we push your offers at peak demand.

Featured store page

An Arabic and English page for your store with clear info, FAQs and tracked links, listed in the right categories and countries.

Why work with us

  • A team experienced in affiliate marketing, content and SEO for Arabic-speaking markets.
  • Arabic-first (RTL) plus English, focused on Saudi Arabia, then the GCC and Egypt.
  • We follow your rules: no code goes live without your approval, no brand bidding, no unauthorised codes.
  • Fast, lightweight, mobile-friendly site with structured data for search.
  • Transparency: we never publish invented traffic numbers — we share real figures once we talk.

How to start

  1. Email partners@kobon.is-a.si with your brand and target countries.
  2. We agree on the model: CPA, exclusive code, or seasonal campaign.
  3. You share your tracking link or official code, and we publish your page within days.
  4. We review performance together and improve offers every season.
