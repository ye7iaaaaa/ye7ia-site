Privacy & cookies

Kobon’s privacy and cookies policy.

This is a static website. You don’t need an account to browse, and we don’t sell your data.

Cookies

We don’t set tracking or advertising cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read most, in aggregate. It doesn’t use cookies or track you across sites.

Forms and email

The partnership and alerts forms are submitted via Formspree to our team inbox at partners@kobon.is-a.si. We only use your details to reply to you, and we don’t sell them.

Store links

Some links may be affiliate links. Clicking them takes you to an external store with its own privacy policy. See the affiliate disclosure and terms.
