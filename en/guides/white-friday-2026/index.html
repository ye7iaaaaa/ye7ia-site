White Friday 2026 in Saudi Arabia and the UAE: dates and how to prepare

When does White Friday 2026 start? What we know about Amazon and noon dates, and how to prepare your list and check a discount before you buy.

Updated: 9 November 20264 min readBy the Kobon team

The short version

  • Black Friday falls on Friday 27 November 2026. Cyber Monday is 30 November.
  • At the time of writing, neither Amazon (Saudi Arabia, UAE) nor noon had announced official 2026 dates.
  • In 2025, Amazon.ae ran White Friday from 20 to 30 November and Amazon.sa from 21 to 30 November.
  • Start a list and record today’s prices now, so you can judge real discounts when the sale opens.

What is White Friday?

White Friday is the name many Gulf retailers give to Black Friday, the Friday after US Thanksgiving. Amazon uses “White Friday” across the region, noon calls its event “Yellow Friday”, and other stores pick their own colour. Whatever the label, it’s no longer a single day here. Over the past few years it has grown into a week or more of back-to-back promotions.

When does White Friday 2026 start?

The only fixed date is the Friday itself: 27 November 2026. Retailers haven’t confirmed their windows yet. Amazon.sa’s seller page says the event will take place towards the end of November, with final dates released closer to the time.

For reference only, here is how last year played out:

Retailer2025 window
Amazon.ae (White Friday)20–30 November
Amazon.sa (White Friday)21–30 November, with early access for Prime members
noon (Yellow Friday)20–30 November

If the pattern holds, expect the big deals to open about a week before the Friday and run to the end of the month. That’s an educated guess, not an announcement. We’ll update this guide as soon as the stores publish their dates.

Build your list before the sale

Impulse buying is what eats most of the savings. Before the deals start:

  1. Write a short list of what you actually need, in order of priority.
  2. Record today’s price for each item at two or three stores, and screenshot the page with the date. That’s your benchmark.
  3. Set a budget ceiling, especially if you plan to pay in instalments.
  4. Add items to your wish list in each app. Some stores notify you when a price drops.
  5. Update your address and payment card in advance. Limited deals sell out fast.

Is the discount real?

A big percentage on the tag isn’t proof of a big saving. Compare the sale price with the price you recorded before the season, not with the “was” price the store shows. If the same product was cheaper elsewhere a month ago, the deal is weaker than it looks.

In Saudi Arabia, sales need a licence from the Ministry of Commerce. You can check a licence by scanning the barcode printed on it, or through the Ministry’s document verification service. It takes seconds and protects you from unlicensed “sales”.

Bank offers and payment

Banks often add instant discounts or cashback on selected cards during the season, and many are capped per card or per customer. Read the terms before you pay, and check whether the bank offer stacks with a discount code. If you plan to split the payment with Tabby or Tamara, confirm the bank offer doesn’t exclude instalment payments. Our buy-now-pay-later guide covers the details.

Delivery and returns

Order volumes peak at the end of November, so delivery can take longer. If you’re buying a gift for a specific date, order early. Check the return policy before paying, since some sale items are final or have different return terms. In Saudi Arabia, the E-Commerce Law gives consumers seven days from delivery to cancel a purchase, provided the item hasn’t been used, with some exceptions. Our returns guide explains how it works.

Common questions

Is White Friday cheaper than 11.11?

Not necessarily. Some products drop further on 11.11, others on White Friday. Note the price at both and compare. Don’t assume the bigger event is always cheaper.

Do I need Prime for Amazon’s deals?

Most deals are open to everyone, but Amazon gave Prime members in Saudi Arabia early access in 2025 and reserves some deals for them. Decide on Prime based on how you use it all year, not for one sale.
