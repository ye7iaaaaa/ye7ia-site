11.11 in the Gulf: what Singles’ Day is and how to make the most of it

Where 11.11 comes from, when Gulf retailers run it, and how to weigh it against White Friday a few weeks later.

Updated: 9 November 20263 min readBy the Kobon team

The short version

  • 11.11 falls on Wednesday 11 November 2026.
  • It started in China as “Singles’ Day”. Alibaba turned it into the country’s biggest online shopping event, and Gulf retailers picked it up.
  • In 2025, noon ran its 11.11 sale from 7 to 12 November in the UAE and Saudi Arabia.
  • It lands about two weeks before White Friday, so compare before you buy anything big.

Where 11.11 comes from

The date is no accident: four ones in a row came to stand for being single, and in China 11 November became Singles’ Day. Alibaba turned it into a huge online shopping festival, and retailers around the world followed. In the Gulf it has become one of November’s headline events, and for many shoppers it now marks the real start of sale season.

How it runs in the Gulf

It’s rarely just one day. In 2025, for example, noon’s 11.11 deals in the UAE and Saudi Arabia started on 7 November and ran until 12 November, according to local media. Fashion, electronics and beauty stores join in with their own promotions. Some open at midnight and last 24 hours; others stretch over several days.

Because every store sets its own timing, don’t plan around the 11th alone. Follow the apps you care about and turn on their notifications a few days ahead.

11.11 or White Friday?

With White Friday about two weeks later, the obvious question is whether to buy now or wait. There’s no single answer, but a few rules of thumb help:

  • Small, repeat purchases such as skincare or everyday clothing: if the price beats what you’ve seen before, there’s little reason to wait.
  • Big-ticket electronics and appliances: note the 11.11 price and compare it with White Friday. If you buy now, know the return policy, and don’t assume the store will refund a later price drop. Few do.
  • Limited-stock items: popular products can sell out before White Friday arrives.

Before you check out

  1. Check the benchmark price. Compare the sale price with what the item cost two weeks ago, not just the crossed-out price.
  2. Read the code’s terms. Minimum spend, maximum discount, excluded categories, and whether it works on items already on sale.
  3. Work out the final cost. Shipping, VAT, and customs if the store is abroad.
  4. Check who the seller is on marketplaces. A third-party seller may have a different return policy from the platform.

International stores on 11.11

Plenty of international stores run 11.11 deals, and some ship straight to the Gulf. Before you jump on a big discount from abroad, add up shipping, import VAT and any customs duty, which can narrow the gap quickly. Our shipping and customs guide covers the thresholds in Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

Common questions

Does 11.11 only run on 11 November?

No. Many stores start a few days before and finish after, as noon did in 2025 with deals from 7 to 12 November. Follow the stores you care about rather than waiting for the day itself.

Do 11.11 codes work on sale items?

It depends on each code’s terms. Many stores don’t allow a code on top of a sale price; some make an exception for the event. Read the terms, then compare the final price both ways.

Can I return something bought on 11.11?

The normal return policy applies unless the store says otherwise, and some event deals are final sale. In Saudi Arabia, the E-Commerce Law gives you seven days from delivery to cancel if the item is unused, with exceptions.
