Our story

Why we built Kobon

We’re an affiliate marketing team with experience across the Gulf and international markets. Working with online stores, we saw the problem from both sides: shoppers losing time on codes that don’t work, and brands losing trust to sites that will publish anything.

Kobon is our answer: official offers held to a clear standard, run by people who know how campaigns are built and measured.

Our principles

Four principles we hold to

Official only

We only publish offers issued by the store or an approved partner programme.

Open about commission

We tell you when we may earn a commission. Your price never changes.

Your time matters

Fewer offers, each with its terms and last-checked date.

Brand safety

No brand-name bidding, no leaked codes, no inflated claims.
