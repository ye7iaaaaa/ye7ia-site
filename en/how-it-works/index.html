The Kobon standard

Four checks
before any offer goes live

An offer that fails any check doesn’t go live.

1

Source

We confirm the offer comes from the store or an approved partner programme.

Example: a code from the store’s official offers page, or from the dashboard of its affiliate network.

2

Terms

We record minimum spend, maximum discount, countries and exclusions.

Example: “Minimum SAR 150, maximum discount SAR 100, electronics excluded.”

3

Test

We try the code on a real basket whenever we can.

Example: we add a product to the basket and check the discount appears before payment.

4

Monitor

We remove expired offers and update the last-checked date.

Example: if the store ends the offer we take it down, and we update the last-checked date on every review.
