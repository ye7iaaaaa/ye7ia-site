How we verify codes

  1. We collect codes from affiliate networks or the store itself.
  2. We review expiry dates and visible terms.
  3. We update the “verified” date when we re-check.
  4. Expired codes are removed or clearly marked.

We cannot guarantee every code works for every shopper — terms vary by cart, country, and account.
