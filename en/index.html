FAQ

Quick answers

What is Kobon?

Kobon is a guide to official coupons and deals for shoppers in the Gulf and Egypt, covering both local and international stores. It’s a new platform focused on official offers only.

Why are there so few offers right now?

Because we never publish a code we can’t trace to its source. Offers go live as soon as official partner programmes with stores are approved.

Do you cover international stores?

Yes. We list offers from international stores that ship to the Gulf and worldwide, and every offer shows which countries it covers.

Does Kobon earn a commission?

A store may pay us a commission when you buy through our links. It costs you nothing extra and never changes the price.

What if a code doesn’t work?

Check the offer’s terms first. If it still fails, let us know from the contact page and we’ll look into it within one business day.
