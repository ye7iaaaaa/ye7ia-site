FAQ

Got a question?

What is Kobon?

Kobon is a deals and discount-code guide for Saudi Arabia, the Gulf and Egypt, run by an affiliate marketing team with experience across the Gulf and Egypt. We only publish offers from official sources.

Why aren’t there many offers yet?

Because we never publish a code we can’t trace to its source. Offers are added as soon as official partnerships with stores go live.

Does Kobon earn a commission?

We may earn a commission from the store when you buy through a partner link, at no extra cost to you. Our affiliate disclosure page explains this.

A code didn’t work. What should I do?

Check the terms on the offer card. If it still doesn’t work, message us from the contact page and we’ll review it the same business day.
