# Affiliate program join requirements (reference)

Internal notes for kobon.is-a.si operators. Compiled from **public official docs** only.
Do **not** sign up or contact networks from the hourly routine. Re-check sources before applying.

Last reviewed: 2026-11-07 (Cairo).

## Admitad (publisher)

Sources:
- [How to sign up for Admitad as a publisher](https://support.mitgo.com/hc/en-us/articles/33314787170578-How-to-sign-up-for-Admitad-as-a-publisher)
- [Why did the advertiser reject my ad space?](https://support.mitgo.com/hc/en-us/articles/33314198364946-Why-did-the-advertiser-reject-my-ad-space)
- Admitad publisher Terms: registration available to persons aged **18+**

Public requirements / expectations:
- Age 18+; accept publisher Terms of Use and personal-data consent.
- Create an **ad space** that is a completed, legal, copyright-compliant site (under-construction sites are commonly refused).
- Join each merchant program separately; advertisers may reject for: rules mismatch, non-target audience/region, insufficient traffic info, low audience, prohibited traffic (e.g. gambling/adult), doorway pages, copyright issues.
- Describe traffic sources and promotion methods when applying to a program.
- Partner-catalog programs may require verification, matching declared site type, privacy policy / imprint (region-dependent), and no agency/network doorway abuse.

## ArabClicks (affiliate / publisher)

Sources:
- [Affiliates support center](https://www.arabclicks.com/support/publishers/)
- [Minimum traffic for approval-required offers](https://www.arabclicks.com/faqs/how-many-followers-do-i-need-to-promote-offers-that-require-approval/)
- [Terms & Conditions](https://www.arabclicks.com/terms-and-conditions/)

Public requirements / expectations:
- Free to join as an affiliate; need an **active** website/blog or social channel you control.
- No minimum audience to **create** an account; many open offers need no approval.
- Offers/coupons that **require approval** use platform thresholds (examples published by ArabClicks):
  - Website: 1,000 sessions / month
  - Instagram: 2,000 followers
  - Facebook Page: 8,000 likes
  - YouTube: 1,000 subscribers
  - TikTok: 1,000 followers
  - Telegram: 500 subscribers
  - (other platforms listed on their FAQ)
- Accurate identity/contact on application; ArabClicks may accept or reject at its discretion.
- Until ~$100 earnings (within their stated window), approval-required requests may be capped (e.g. 2).

## Boostiny (ArabyAds network)

Sources:
- [Boostiny Terms & Conditions](https://boostiny.com/terms-conditions/)
- Public site: [boostiny.com](https://boostiny.com/) (publisher login/register flows)

Public requirements / expectations:
- Publisher must be an **entity or individual aged 18+**.
- Registration information must be authentic, accurate, current, and complete; network may verify and restrict/terminate for misleading data.
- Personal-capacity registration is for **business** use of the network (not as a consumer).
- Campaign participation is governed by separate engagement / IO terms and each campaign’s rules (tracking links/coupons must stay unaltered).
- Social influencers may be directed to a separate influencer product (e.g. RevGate) rather than the classic publisher form — check the live signup page before applying.
- Geographic focus: MENA brands/publishers (Egypt, KSA, Gulf, North Africa commonly mentioned in public materials).

## Other networks named on the marketers page

Awin / Impact / Amazon Associates: each merchant program has its **own** creative, region, and disclosure rules. Use only the official publisher help centers for that network before any application. Do not invent traffic stats when applying.

## Site readiness checklist (for when Mario decides to apply)

- Live HTTPS site with clear Saudi/GCC coupon focus (not a doorway).
- Privacy, Terms, Affiliate disclosure, and Contact pages published.
- Real (non-example) store pages before pitching programs that reject empty sites.
- Honest traffic description; never fabricate stats.
